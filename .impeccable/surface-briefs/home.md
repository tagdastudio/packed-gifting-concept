# Surface brief: home

Mode: Persuade

## Scope

The home page of a fictional gifting house, **Packed**, built as a configurable showcase template for gift, hamper and corporate-gifting businesses. It must read as a real shop, not a theme demo. Demo details are illustrative; no real client's name, contact, address, photography or claims appear.

## Audience and action

A gift-giver on a phone, close to an occasion, deciding whether this shop understands the gift they need; and a corporate buyer checking whether the shop can handle volume. The page must make both paths obvious within two screens. Primary action: start building a hamper or open the hampers. Secondary: send a corporate brief or visit the store.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A gift-packing room, not a catalogue. The page is the counter: a chest is unpacked in the first viewport, then the same materials become shelves, packing lists, a hamper builder and a WhatsApp brief. It refuses the centred hero, three icon cards and a product grid.

OWN-WORLD: Deep olive shop walls, kraft counter, cream tissue, saffron and vermilion ribbon, brass hardware, chocolate and newsprint. Petrona for display, Host Grotesk for body and interface, Kalam only for notes. A chest with a brass latch, tins with paper labels, a printed packing list, wax seal, cut ribbon ends. Flat colour bands and real contact shadows; no glass, gradients or neon.

STORY: The visitor understands that Packed fills boxes by hand, can make one for their person or a company, and can answer on WhatsApp. They believe the gifts are specific and the process is short. They act by opening the builder, opening a hamper, or sending a brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440×900 dark olive room. Wordmark top left, four links and a small WhatsApp action top right. A low chest sits right of centre on a kraft counter under one warm lamp, sealed with a red wax stamp and tied with ribbon; a kraft tag hangs from the knot. Headline left in cream: “Some things are worth packing properly.” Two actions beneath: Build a hamper, See the hampers. A thin line under them: “Packed by hand in Mysuru. Sent across India on request.” On a phone, the headline sits above the chest, both actions remain above the fold, and the tag is readable.

FORM: The chosen form is the packing-room counter, first on my ordered list, pinned by the user's words “gift box wrapped with paper and ribbon vibe” and the approved chest-unwrap experience. seed: none (pinned) — “gift box wrapped with paper and ribbon vibe.”

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Unresolved

- Exact hamper names and contents are illustrative until the studio supplies a client's range.
- Photography is optional; drawn specimen plates are the fallback and must look finished.
- Corporate minimums and delivery windows are placeholders, clearly labelled.
